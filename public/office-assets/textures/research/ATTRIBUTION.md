# Research room materials

`floor-color.jpg`, `floor-normal.jpg`, and `floor-roughness.jpg` are the unmodified
1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps from **Large Floor Tiles 02**
by **Rob Tuytel**, distributed by Poly Haven under **CC0 1.0**.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/large_floor_tiles_02
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license
- CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These files are served locally; rendering does not contact the asset provider.

`oak-color.jpg`, `oak-normal.jpg`, and `oak-roughness.jpg` are the unmodified
1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps from **Oak Veneer 01**
by **Jenelle van Heerden**, distributed by Poly Haven under **CC0 1.0**.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01
- Source files: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/oak_veneer_01/
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license
- CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source spans 1.8 meters; the room maps the grain along the furniture width.
The image pixels are unchanged. Rotation, scale, and relief strength are set in the renderer.
